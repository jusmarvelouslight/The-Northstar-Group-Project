- **30-day Return Window**: From the date of delivery.
- **Conditions**: Item must be unused and in original packaging.
- **Steps**:
  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to "Orders."
  3. Select "Return Item."
  4. Print the prepaid FedEx label provided.
  5. Drop the package off at any FedEx location.
- **Refund Process**: Typically processed within 5-7 business days after receipt of the return.
- **Exchanges**: Permitted for the same item in a different size or colour.
- **Final Sale Items**: Not eligible for return.
