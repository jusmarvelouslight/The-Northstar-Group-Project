## Section 2: Return Policy
- **30-Day Window**: Customers can return items within 30 days from delivery.
- **Condition**: Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- **Return Steps**: 
  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to 'Orders'.
  3. Select 'Return Item'.
  4. Print the prepaid FedEx label.
  5. Drop off at any FedEx location.
- **Refund Process**: Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days upon receipt.
- **Exchanges**: Items can be exchanged for the same item in a different size or color.
- **Final Sale Items**: Not eligible for return.
