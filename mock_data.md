## 2. Return Policy
- Customers have a 30-day window from delivery to initiate returns.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- Steps to return:
  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to Orders.
  3. Select "Return Item."
  4. Print the prepaid FedEx label.
  5. Drop the item at any FedEx location.
- Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received.
- Exchanges are available for the same item in a different size or color; final sale items are not eligible for return.
